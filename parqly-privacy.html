ParQly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

ParQly ("the app") helps you find parkings, fuel stations, EV charging points and car washes around the world. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and what we never do with it.

The short version

  • No account. ParQly works without signing up or logging in.
  • No ads. The app contains no advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and we do not build profiles of our users.
  • Your saved places, timers, car photos and settings stay on your device.

Location

With your permission, ParQly uses your device's location to show parkings and stations near you and to center the map. To answer "what is near this point?", the app sends the coordinates of the area you are viewing as a map query to the public OpenStreetMap services (the Overpass API and Nominatim geocoding). Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap / MapLibre tile servers, and satellite imagery from Esri. If you open the weather feature, the coordinates are sent to the Open-Meteo weather service. These requests contain the coordinates needed to answer the query and nothing that identifies you personally; they are handled under each provider's own privacy policy. You can also use ParQly without location permission by searching for a city by name.

Community radar reports

ParQly Pro includes speed-camera ("radar") alerts. Camera locations come from OpenStreetMap. If you choose to report a camera, the app sends only the coordinates and the report type to our server (a Cloudflare Worker) so other users can see it. Reports are anonymous: no name, account, device ID or any other identifier is attached.

Data stored on your device

The following is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded to us:

  • Favorite (saved) places
  • Parking timers and parking history
  • "Find My Car" location and any photo you attach to it
  • Ticket photos and spot notes
  • Cars you add (name, plate, photo)
  • Downloaded offline cities and app settings (language, map style)

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Android's standard app backup may include it in your own device backup, under your control.

Permissions

  • Location — to find parkings near you (optional).
  • Notifications — parking-timer reminders and radar alerts.
  • Camera / photos — only when you attach a photo to a parking spot or ticket.
  • Exact alarms & battery exemption — so timer notifications arrive on time even when the phone is asleep.

Children

ParQly is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.
